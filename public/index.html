Co-op Climb

A multiplayer platformer for up to 15 players.
Work together — stand on pressure plates, stack on each other's heads, and get everyone past the obstacles.

Controls

  • A/← move left
  • D/→ move right
  • Space/W/↑ jump
  • R respawn at checkpoint (if stuck)
  • Shift+R vote-restart the level
